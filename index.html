01 / About Us

Building a more accessible television experience.

DCM Streams is a digital media initiative developed by The Gentlemen Joint Projects (Pty) Ltd, a South African registered company.

Our objective is to establish a convenient television distribution platform that connects audiences with authorised local and international programming through a flexible digital viewing experience.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and accessible digital television destination, bringing diverse programming to audiences across supported devices and markets.

Our Mission

To simplify access to live television through flexible viewing options, reliable technology and responsible content distribution.

Our Approach

To build sustainable relationships with content partners while balancing consumer affordability, commercial viability and the protection of intellectual property.

Our Initial Market

South Africa is our intended initial commercial market. Future geographic expansion will be evaluated according to market demand, regulatory requirements and the territorial rights granted by our content partners.

02 / Business Model

Flexible access. Greater convenience.

Our proposed consumer model is built around registered accounts and flexible prepaid viewing access rather than mandatory recurring subscription commitments.

01

Daily Viewing Access

Short-duration viewing access designed for customers seeking immediate entertainment without long-term commitments.

02

Weekly Viewing Access

Flexible access for customers who prefer to manage their entertainment expenditure on a weekly basis.

03

Monthly Viewing Access

Extended viewing access for customers seeking ongoing entertainment without mandatory automatic subscription renewal.

Accessible Payment Options

Our payment strategy is intended to support convenient prepaid purchasing through approved voucher and digital payment providers. Additional payment methods may be introduced as integrations and commercial approvals become available.

Audience Development

Selected free-to-view channels, introductory viewing trials and promotional access may be introduced where permitted by content partners and applicable licensing agreements.

Commercial Sustainability

Our business model is designed to balance affordability for consumers with sustainable commercial opportunities for participating content partners. Pricing, package composition and commercial arrangements are evaluated according to content rights, operational requirements and market conditions.

03 / Content Distribution

A diverse television destination.

Our primary launch objective is live linear television distribution through internet-based streaming technology. We welcome discussions with established broadcasters, independent content owners, FAST channel operators and authorised distribution representatives.

News & Current Affairs Sports Entertainment Movies Documentaries Music Lifestyle Children & Family Education Faith & Inspirational Community Television International Programming

Linear Television

Our initial focus is scheduled live television channels delivered through authorised internet-based distribution arrangements.

FAST Channels

We welcome opportunities involving free ad-supported streaming television, subject to agreed advertising, distribution and commercial requirements.

Future On-Demand Services

Movies and television series on demand form part of our longer-term development strategy and will be considered under appropriate separate content rights.

Authorised Content Only

Channel availability will depend on applicable permissions, contractual agreements, territorial rights and technical readiness. Publicly accessible streaming feeds are not treated as automatic permission for commercial redistribution.

06 / Technology

One platform. Multiple viewing environments.

DCM Streams is being developed as a multi-platform digital television service, beginning with a browser-based application and progressing towards dedicated applications for additional device ecosystems.

Mobile & Tablet

Android smartphones and tablets, Apple iPhones and iPads.

Connected Television

Android TV, Google TV, Amazon Fire TV, Fire Stick and Apple TV.

Smart Television

Samsung Smart TVs and LG Smart TVs, subject to platform requirements.

Desktop & Web

Windows computers, Mac computers and supported modern web browsers.

Streaming Integration

  • HLS-based live television delivery
  • Adaptive streaming where supported
  • Electronic programme guide integration
  • Primary and backup stream support
  • Playback recovery and reconnection
  • Device-appropriate viewing interfaces

Technical Collaboration

We welcome discussions regarding authorised streaming feeds, delivery specifications, programme guide metadata, advertising integration, content protection, geographic restrictions and operational support.

Technical compatibility and any additional protection requirements will be assessed during partner onboarding.

Device compatibility and technical capabilities described above represent our intended platform roadmap. Availability will depend on development progress, testing, platform approvals and individual content partner requirements.

07 / Compliance & Governance

Responsible distribution by design.

Our objective is to establish a distribution environment that respects intellectual property, contractual obligations, consumer protection and applicable regulatory requirements.

Content Rights

Commercial content distribution will be subject to appropriate authorisation from rights holders or their appointed distribution representatives.

Territorial Restrictions

Geographic availability and distribution conditions will be governed by applicable licensing agreements and supported technical controls.

Account & Access Protection

Our platform architecture incorporates registered customer accounts and controlled viewing access, with additional protection measures evaluated according to partner requirements.

Payment Integrity

Payment integrations are being developed around trusted provider verification and controlled activation of viewing access. Commercial payment availability remains subject to provider approval.

Privacy & Data Protection

Our approach is intended to support responsible data handling, restricted access to customer information and applicable privacy obligations, including South Africa's POPIA.

Regulatory Readiness

Applicable regulatory classifications, licensing requirements and commercial approvals are being assessed as part of our pre-launch preparation.

10 / Frequently Asked Questions

Information for prospective partners.

Who operates DCM Streams?

DCM Streams is being developed by The Gentlemen Joint Projects (Pty) Ltd, a South African registered company.

Is DCM Streams currently available to the public?

No. The consumer streaming platform is currently under development. This website provides corporate and partnership information.

What is the intended business model?

Our proposed model provides flexible prepaid viewing access through registered customer accounts, without mandatory recurring subscription commitments. Selected free and promotional access may be introduced where appropriate rights are granted.

Which territories will DCM Streams serve?

South Africa is our intended initial market. Additional territories may be considered subject to applicable rights, regulations and commercial opportunities.

What commercial arrangements are available?

We welcome discussions involving authorised free-to-view distribution, revenue sharing, referral partnerships, performance-based arrangements and individually negotiated licensing models. Specific commercial terms are discussed privately.

Can broadcasters promote their channels?

Our roadmap includes approved channel branding, promotional content, featured placements and collaborative marketing opportunities, subject to partner permissions.

Will content partners receive performance reports?

Dedicated partner reporting is planned. Available metrics, reporting frequency, commercial information and access permissions will be determined through implementation and individual agreements.

Can DCM Streams integrate existing streaming feeds?

Our platform is being developed to support authorised internet-based streaming feeds. Delivery formats, metadata, advertising and protection requirements will be evaluated during technical onboarding.

Does DCM Streams require exclusive distribution rights?

Exclusivity is not a general prerequisite for partnership discussions. Distribution conditions will be evaluated and negotiated individually.

How can a broadcaster or distributor become a partner?

Prospective partners are invited to contact our business development team with information about their content portfolio, available territories and distribution requirements.
